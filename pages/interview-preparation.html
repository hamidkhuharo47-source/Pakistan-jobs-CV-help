Interview Preparation

Prepare clear, truthful examples and practise explaining your skills.

Before the interview

  • Research the organisation and role.
  • Read the job description and identify required skills.
  • Prepare examples from study, work, volunteering or projects.
  • Plan your travel or test your video-call setup.

Use the STAR method

Describe the Situation, Task, Action and Result. Keep answers focused and truthful. If you do not know an answer, explain how you would find out.
