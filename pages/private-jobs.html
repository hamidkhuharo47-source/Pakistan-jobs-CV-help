How to apply safely

  • Open the official employer link provided with each vacancy.
  • Read the complete job description and eligibility criteria.
  • Check the closing date before submitting your application.
  • Prepare an accurate, job-specific CV.
  • Never pay someone who promises to guarantee you a job.
  • Never share your passwords or one-time verification codes.
