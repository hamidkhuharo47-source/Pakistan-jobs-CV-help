Privacy Policy

Learn how we handle your information and protect your privacy.

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Welcome to Pakistan Jobs & CV Help. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, how it is used, and how third-party services may process your information.

1. Information We Collect

We may receive personal information when you voluntarily contact us through our Contact Us form. This information may include:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • The message or enquiry you submit.

We do not ask visitors to provide personal information simply to browse our job information and career guidance pages.

2. Contact Form and Email

Our contact form uses FormSubmit, a third-party form processing service, to deliver messages to our email address.

When you submit the form, your name, email address and message are transmitted through FormSubmit and delivered to our Gmail inbox.

These messages may be retained in our email account for communication and record-keeping purposes. FormSubmit may also process submissions according to its own privacy policy and terms.

3. CV Builder and Personal Information

Our CV Builder allows visitors to enter information such as their name, contact details, education, skills and work experience to prepare a CV.

The CV Builder processes the entered information in your browser to generate a printable CV. It is not designed to upload CV details to our website server.

Visitors should avoid entering sensitive information that is not necessary for creating a CV. Please also be careful when saving or sharing your completed CV.

4. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use basic browser technologies to display pages and provide website functionality. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical information associated with website requests.

We do not currently claim to operate our own advertising or analytics tracking system. If additional services are introduced, this policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

We may apply for Google AdSense or use other advertising services in the future.

If advertising services are introduced, third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display advertisements, measure advertising performance and provide relevant advertising.

Google's advertising practices and available privacy controls are explained in Google's Privacy Policy and advertising settings.

This section describes possible future services and does not mean that Google AdSense is currently active on this website.

6. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to government departments, employers, job portals and other external websites.

We do not control the privacy practices or content of these external websites. Visitors should review the privacy policies of third-party websites before providing personal information.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information that visitors voluntarily submit to us. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Please avoid sending passwords, financial information or other highly sensitive personal details through our contact form.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website provides general job information and career resources. It is not specifically directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our services.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may choose not to submit personal information through our contact form. You may also contact us to request information about personal details you have submitted or to request correction or deletion where reasonably possible and legally appropriate.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website features, services or applicable requirements change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised update date.
