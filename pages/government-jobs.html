Government Jobs in Pakistan

Find official public-sector recruitment information and learn how to verify advertisements.

Where to check

  • Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC) official website
  • Provincial public service commissions, such as SPSC, PPSC, KPPSC and BPSC
  • Official department and government websites

Before applying

Read the original advertisement carefully. Check eligibility, age limits, domicile requirements, fee instructions, deadline and application method. Never rely on an unverified social media post.
