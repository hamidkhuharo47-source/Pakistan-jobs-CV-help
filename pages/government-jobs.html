How to Apply

  1. Visit the official Karachi Port Trust careers website.
  2. Download the prescribed application form.
  3. Read the original recruitment advertisement carefully.
  4. Prepare your CV, attested educational and experience documents, CNIC copy, domicile and recent photograph.
  5. Follow the advertisement's instructions for the non-refundable Rs. 100 pay order.
  6. Send the completed application by mail or courier to the address specified in the advertisement before the deadline.
