Career Tips

Practical guidance for job applications, CV writing and career development.

Getting your first job with limited experience

Include relevant coursework, projects, internships, volunteering and practical skills. Explain what you did and what you learned without claiming paid experience you do not have.

Tailor your CV

Read the job advertisement and highlight the skills you genuinely have that match the role. Use clear headings and concise bullet points.

Discussing salary

Research a reasonable range, consider the responsibilities and answer professionally. Do not invent a competing offer.
