Career Tips

Practical guidance for job applications, CV writing and career development.

Getting your first job with limited experience

Include relevant coursework, projects, internships, volunteering and practical skills. Explain what you did and what you learned without claiming paid experience you do not have.

Tailor your CV

Read the job advertisement and highlight the skills you genuinely have that match the role. Use clear headings and concise bullet points.

Discussing salary

Research a reasonable range, consider the responsibilities and answer professionally. Do not invent a competing offer.

How to write a professional CV

A good CV should be clear, organised and easy to read. Include the following sections where relevant:

  • Your full name and professional contact details.
  • A short professional summary or career objective.
  • Your educational qualifications.
  • Relevant work experience, internships or projects.
  • Computer, communication and job-related skills.
  • Relevant courses, certificates and training.

Use a simple layout, check spelling and keep information accurate. Avoid adding unnecessary personal information or skills you cannot demonstrate.

Important computer skills for office jobs in Pakistan

Computer skills can be useful when applying for administrative, accounts and office assistant positions.

  • MS Word: Creating and formatting professional documents.
  • MS Excel: Working with spreadsheets, basic formulas and records.
  • Email: Writing professional messages and attaching documents.
  • Internet research: Finding and checking relevant information.
  • File management: Organising documents and folders on a computer.

Practise these skills regularly and mention only the tools you can actually use.

How to find genuine job opportunities

Use official employer career pages, recognised recruitment platforms and verified newspaper advertisements. Before applying:

  • Check the employer's official website.
  • Confirm the job title, location and eligibility requirements.
  • Read the application instructions and closing date.
  • Check whether the vacancy is still accepting applications.
  • Keep a copy of the advertisement and your application.

Do not rely only on social media posts or forwarded messages when checking whether a vacancy is genuine.

How to apply for jobs by email

When an employer accepts applications by email, follow its instructions carefully.

  1. Use a professional email address.
  2. Write a clear subject containing the job title.
  3. Introduce yourself briefly in the email.
  4. Mention the position you are applying for.
  5. Attach your updated CV and other requested documents.
  6. Check the recipient's email address before sending.

Example subject: Application for Office Assistant Position — Your Name

Keep your email polite and concise. Do not send documents that the employer has not requested unless they are relevant to the application.

Preparing for government and banking jobs

Government departments and banks may use different recruitment processes. Read each advertisement carefully because eligibility requirements and selection stages vary.

  • Check the required education and experience.
  • Review age limits, domicile and quota requirements where applicable.
  • Prepare the documents requested in the advertisement.
  • Practise relevant subject knowledge and basic computer skills.
  • Prepare for written tests, interviews or other selection stages if required.
  • Submit your application before the official deadline.

Always follow the specific recruitment instructions published by the relevant organisation.

How to avoid fake job advertisements and recruitment scams

Job seekers should be careful when responding to unfamiliar recruiters or online vacancy announcements.

  • Be suspicious of guaranteed jobs in exchange for money.
  • Verify vacancies through the employer's official website or contact details.
  • Never share passwords, OTPs or banking PINs with recruiters.
  • Do not send money to personal accounts for promised employment.
  • Be cautious about requests for unnecessary identity or financial documents.
  • Report suspicious advertisements to the relevant platform or authorities.

Legitimate recruitment procedures vary, so check any required application fee or payment directly against the employer's official instructions.

Keep developing your career

Career development takes time. Continue learning, improving your communication and computer skills, updating your CV and gaining relevant practical experience. Small improvements can help you prepare for better opportunities.
